The Situation

You are the Regional Vice President of Human Resources for XYZ Corp, based in Hong Kong. Your company manufactures components for toys and, recently classified, for military applications — specifically propellers for surveillance drones used at U.S. military bases.

The U.S. government has directed you to relocate production to the United States. The team working on these parts must be removed from China.

The complication: Three days ago, BorgWarner executives were arrested in Shanghai while communicating with staff about similar restrictions. They remain under house arrest.

Your Technology Security Department has told you: no internet, no email, meetings in person only, nothing in writing. Assume all digital communication is compromised.

Tomorrow morning, you will hike the MacLehose Trail with the Regional VP of Operations and the Regional President — a chance to plan without surveillance. Later, you will present your recommendations via secure app.

You have 30 years in China, speak the language, and have seen a lot. Your colleagues are looking to you for the plan.
