Assignment Instructions

Overview

You are about to participate in a crisis simulation that will test your understanding of international business strategy, cross-cultural management, and risk assessment in a high-stakes scenario.

In this simulation, you play the role of the Regional Vice President of Human Resources for XYZ Corporation, an American manufacturing company with operations in China. Your company has been directed by the U.S. government to relocate defense-related production out of China. You must demonstrate your understanding of the situation by answering questions from headquarters via a secure communication channel.

Learning Objectives

By completing this simulation, you will demonstrate your ability to:

  1. Assess risk in complex international business environments
  2. Apply cultural frameworks (Trompenaars, GLOBE, face, guanxi) to real-world scenarios
  3. Synthesize historical precedents to inform strategic decision-making
  4. Balance competing demands from multiple stakeholders and sovereigns
  5. Make decisions under time pressure with incomplete information

Preparation Requirements

Before attempting the simulation, you must thoroughly study:

  1. The XYZ Corp Case Brief — The scenario, your role, and the 100 questions you may be asked
  2. The Marion Wheeler Lives Consulting Memo — Cultural frameworks, historical precedents, and strategic analysis

You should be prepared to answer any of the 100 questions in the case brief. The simulation will randomly select 20 questions for you to answer.
